What's On the Counter

What’s In the Case

Everything is made in-house in small batches. Flavours rotate through the week — the case looks a little different every visit.

Classic Fudge

Milk and dark chocolate, cookies and cream, vanilla walnut, maple caramel, butterscotch, coffee mocha, mint, Irish cream, strawberry and matcha — twelve loaves, cut to whatever size you want.

Brittle, Bark & Toffee

Peanut, pecan and cashew brittle, white/milk/dark chocolate bark, English toffee and sea foam.

Caramel & Dipped

Chocolate-dipped strawberries, caramel apples on a stick, caramel squares and hand-decorated lollies.

Why Olde World

Small Batches, Cut in Front of You

Nothing here is pre-packed on a shelf. The fudge sits in the case as full loaves, and it gets cut, weighed and wrapped while you wait — a single slice or a slab, whatever you actually want.
